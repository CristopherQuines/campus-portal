About the Portal

Portal Purpose: This portal is built with Django to provide students with fast, accessible campus information.

Intended Users: Designed specifically for IT and College of Computer Studies (CCS) students, faculty members, and academic advisers.

Accessibility Feature: Implemented high-contrast crimson and light colors, descriptive image alt text, and visible yellow keyboard focus outlines (:focus) so users can easily navigate links using the Tab key.
